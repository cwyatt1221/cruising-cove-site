— Two kinds of stops

Private islands vs commercial ports

Private island Commercial port
Examples Castaway Cay, Lookout Cay Nassau, Cozumel, San Juan, St. Thomas…
Need a paid tour? Usually no — beach + BBQ is the day Often yes if you want a beach club or far activity
Transport decisions Tram / walk on island Walk vs taxi vs booked transfer — first question every time
Biggest risk Sun + overbooking cabanas you don’t need Missing all-aboard after an independent tour
— How to use a port day

Work backward from all-aboard

  • Note all-aboard time first (not just “we’re docked until afternoon”). Build a buffer for tenders, traffic, and getting back through security.
  • Decide beach day vs. town day vs. paid excursion before you’re on the pier — switching late usually means paying tourist prices for a rushed version of both.
  • If you want a paid tour, use the Port Adventures filter guide and the Disney vs. independent tradeoff.
  • Commercial ports vary wildly by walking distance from the pier. Distance is the first fact to check — it controls food, shopping, and whether you need a taxi at all.
