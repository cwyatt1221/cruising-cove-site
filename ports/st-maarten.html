Port of call · Eastern Caribbean

St. Maarten.

One island, two sides — Dutch Philipsburg for shopping and beach clubs, French St. Martin for a different lunch scene, and Maho for plane-spotting beaches.

— The day

Pick one job for the day

Choose Philipsburg beach + shopping, a beach club, or Maho / French side outing — not a full island circumnavigation. Confirm all-aboard and pier logistics in Navigator.

Low transfer

Philipsburg boardwalk

Beach chairs, duty-free shopping, and lunch within a short walk or taxi of the pier.

Signature sight

Maho Beach

Plane-spotting over the beach — thrilling for some kids, loud and crowded; know your family’s comfort level.

Food day

French side lunch

A sit-down meal across the border — plan taxi time both ways and don’t stack Maho afterward.

— Disney vs independent

What to book, what to skip

  • Book Disney when: you want a packaged beach club or island tour with the ship-wait cushion.
  • Go independent when: you’re staying in Philipsburg with a clear taxi plan for one add-on.
  • Skip: Maho + French side + full shopping circuit in one call.
  • Skip: standing under aircraft for photos if anyone in your party is noise-sensitive or uneasy.

  • Disney transfer. Useful for farther beach clubs. See Port Adventures.
— Food

What to eat ashore

  • Philipsburg. Boardwalk and downtown options for a quick lunch.
  • French side. Worth it if food is your day’s “job” — not a rushed add-on.
  • Beach clubs. Packages simplify lunch with sandy kids.
— Shopping

What’s worth bringing home

  • Duty-free / jewelry / liquor. Philipsburg is built for it — set a budget before you browse.
  • Pressure pitches. Common near the pier; decline and keep moving.
— Safety

With kids in daylight

  • Stay in tourist corridors; keep valuables zipped in crowded shop streets.
  • Maho: hearing protection idea for sensitive kids; respect barriers and staff instructions.
  • Sun and dehydration still win most “bad day” stories — water and shade.
— Timing

Work backward from all-aboard

  • Maho needs a hard leave-by time for the taxi back.
  • Shopping expands endlessly — set a walk-away alarm.
  • One side of the island per call is enough.
— Priority

The one thing repeats push

If you only do one thing, pick Philipsburg beach + a paced shop stroll — or commit fully to Maho. Half-doing both usually means enjoying neither.
