Castaway Cay.

Disney's original private island: a protected lagoon, three separate beaches, and a complimentary BBQ lunch included in every stop.

— The island

What's there

Castaway Cay is Disney Cruise Line's private island in the Bahamas, and the ship docks directly at a pier — no tender boat required, which means faster, easier access than most cruise-line private islands. The island is split into three beach areas: two family beaches (connected by tram and walking paths) and Serenity Bay, reserved for guests 18 and older. The water throughout is shallow and calm, protected by a breakwater, which makes it easy and safe for young kids.

A complimentary BBQ lunch is served at both the family beaches and Serenity Bay between roughly 11:30am and 2:00pm — grilled chicken, burgers, hot dogs, fish, and sides, with non-alcoholic drinks included. You never have to return to the ship for lunch, though the option is there if you'd rather.

— Free to do

Included activities

  • Pelican Plunge — a free floating inflatable water play structure just off the family beach
  • Snorkeling in the designated lagoon, including sunken Disney-themed props
  • A 5K running course around the island (free, self-guided)
  • Hammocks, lounge chairs, and umbrellas throughout all three beaches
  • Character meet-and-greets in tropical attire — check the Navigator app for times
  • Massage cabanas at Serenity Bay (booked through the onboard spa, extra cost)
— Worth booking early

Cabanas

Castaway Cay has 20 private cabanas split between the family beach and Serenity Bay — roughly 325 square feet each, with lounge chairs, a hammock, a stocked cooler, and complimentary snorkel gear. Family beach cabanas run roughly $450–600/day; Serenity Bay cabanas start around $600+/day. They're genuinely worth it for a large group wanting guaranteed shade and privacy, but they sell out within minutes of the booking window opening — typically 75 days before sailing for the general public, earlier for higher Castaway Club tiers.

— Worth knowing

Planning tips

  • Bring your stateroom key card and comfortable shoes if you plan to run the 5K
  • Beach towels are provided at the pier — no need to pack your own
  • The family beaches get crowded near the tram stop; walking 5–10 minutes further usually finds quieter spots
  • Food is free at both BBQ locations; bar drinks and specialty items are not
