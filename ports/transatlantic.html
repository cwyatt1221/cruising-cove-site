Repositioning · Transatlantic

Transatlantic.

Florida-to-Europe (or reverse) repositioning sailings — lots of sea days, embark and return on different continents, and a crossing rhythm unlike any 3-night Bahamas cruise.

— Overview

A canal of ocean days

Transatlantic Disney sailings move ships between U.S. homeports and Europe for seasonal deployments. Like Panama Canal repositionings, they’re one-way in spirit: different embark and return cities, long stretches at sea, and planning that starts with flights on two continents.

At sea

Sea days are the product

Shows, clubs, pools, Palo, and rest — pack for entertainment pacing and motion care.

Logistics

Open-jaw travel

Fly into one side of the Atlantic and home from the other — book both ends early.

Ports

A few calls, not a port-a-day map

Expect limited port days compared with Caribbean weeklies; confirm the live itinerary.

— Before you book

Age, docs & payment

  • Minimum age: Many repositioning sailings (including Transatlantic) require guests to be at least 1 year old — see the FAQ.
  • Passports: Essentially required for everyone on these itineraries.
  • Final payment: Longer sailings typically use the ~120-day window — deposit & final payment.
  • Seasickness: Open ocean is the point — read seasickness planning.

— Before you book

Planning checklist

  • Entertainment strategy: Map sea days like port days — protect nap/rest windows for kids.
  • Specialty dining: More nights means more Palo chances — still book early.
  • Laundry / packing: Longer sailing = laundry plan or more clothes.
  • Wi-Fi: Decide if you need packages for a long stretch — Wi-Fi guide.
— Priority

The one rule

Book the crossing for the sea days and the repositioning convenience — not for a port-dense Caribbean checklist.
