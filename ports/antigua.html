Antigua.

Classic Eastern Caribbean beach-and-bay day — St. John’s near the pier, calm swim spots around the island, and fort views if you want a short culture stop.

— The day

Pick one job for the day

Antigua is easiest as beach club, St. John’s + fort, or one sail/snorkel. Don’t try to collect every famous beach on a single call.

Most families

Beach club day

Chairs, lunch, and calm water — the default Antigua win.

Low transfer

St. John’s + fort view

Town shopping plus a short historic stop without a long island transfer.

On the water

Catamaran / snorkel

One boat outing with clear return timing — check motion comfort first.

— Disney vs independent

What to book, what to skip

  • Book Disney when: the beach or boat is farther out and you want the ship-wait cushion.
  • Go independent when: you’re staying closer to St. John’s with a known taxi plan.
  • Skip: three beaches in one day — pick one bay and enjoy it.
  • Skip: leaving beach return to “we’ll find a taxi later” without a plan.

— Food

What to eat ashore

  • Beach clubs. Lunch packages are the low-decision option.
  • St. John’s. Casual lunch if you’re doing a town day.
  • Hydration. Treat water like a packing item.
— Shopping

What’s worth bringing home

  • St. John’s. Souvenirs and duty-free browsing — set a budget.
  • Pier vendors. Convenience priced for cruise traffic.
— Safety

With kids in daylight

  • Stick to known clubs and tourist corridors.
  • Sun and reef footwear matter for beach days.
  • Cash for taxis; cards at many tourist spots — carry a backup.
— Timing

Work backward from all-aboard

  • Beach transfers need padding.
  • Be in the return taxi earlier than the map suggests.
  • Town day is the backup if boats cancel.
— Priority

The one thing repeats push

Commit to one beautiful bay and stay. Antigua’s best days are unhurried beach days, not island scavenger hunts.
