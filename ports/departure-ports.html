Departure ports

Start the trip smooth, not scrambled.

Nearest airports, where to stay the night before, and parking, rideshare, and transfer options compared by real cost and time — every major Disney U.S. homeport broken down below.

— Rule one

Stay near the port the night before

For most families, the cheapest “risk” on a Disney cruise is a same-day flight into embarkation. A delay doesn’t just cost a hotel — it can cost the first night of the cruise. Plan to arrive the day before, sleep near the terminal, and take a short morning transfer.

  • Port Canaveral — Orlando / East Coast hub
  • PortMiami — Miami
  • Port Everglades — Fort Lauderdale
  • Galveston · San Diego
— Most common start

Port Canaveral (Orlando area)

The East Coast hub for Bahamian and many Caribbean itineraries, including Wish-class 3- and 4-night sailings and longer Treasure / Fantasy sailings. Disney typically uses the A-side terminals (often Terminal 8 or 10) — confirm your terminal in the Disney Cruise Line app closer to sail day.

Primary airportMCO
MCO drive45–60+ min
Onsite parking~$20/day + tax
Disney transfer~$45 pp one way
— Getting there

Airports compared

Most families fly MCO for schedule and price. Melbourne Orlando (MLB) can shave drive time when the right flights exist — usually fewer of them.

Airport Drive to port Best for Watch-outs
Orlando (MCO) ~45–60 min Most flights, Disney ground transfer desk, more hotel options near airport SR 528 tolls; afternoon thunderstorms and weekend beach traffic can stretch to 75+ minutes
Melbourne Orlando (MLB) ~30–40 min Shorter transfer when your flights line up Fewer nonstops; usually no Disney motorcoach option
Sanford (SFB) ~75–90+ min Some Allegiant / charter fares Longer door-to-port time; plan night-before near the port, not near SFB

From MCO, take the North exit toward SR 528 East (toll). Exit 54A for A terminals (CT-5, 6, 8, 10); Exit 54B for B terminals. Terminal assignments can change — follow your cruise documents.

— Airport to pier

Transfer options (sample all-in)

Compare total dollars for your party size, not the per-person headline. Prices below are planning ranges for MCO ↔ Port Canaveral; confirm current rates before you book.

Option Typical cost Time / stress Wins when…
Disney ground transfer ~$45 pp ages 3+ each way 2 & under free · ~$90 pp round trip Motorcoach; meet at Disney’s airport / hotel desk. Extra wait is normal. Bags handled by you at the airport end. You want zero decisions, especially with kids or a land+sea hotel pickup
Shared shuttle ~$25–45 pp each way Varies by company & season Often cheaper than Disney; may wait for other flights. Book return timing carefully. 3–5 people and you’re fine waiting a bit to save
Rideshare / taxi ~$70–140 one way for the car Surge, tolls, tip, car seats Leaves when you leave. Capacity and car seats are on you. 1–2 adults, or a family that already has seats and calm surge pricing
Private car / van ~$150–280+ one way Meet-and-greet priced higher Door-to-door, fixed time, easier with lots of luggage. Bigger party, early morning flights, or you’d rather prepay certainty
Rental car + port parking Rental days + ~$20/day parking + tax Drop rental at port when possible Maximum flex if you’re driving the Florida coast anyway. You’re touring Central Florida before/after and want own wheels
— Quick math

Family of four, ages 3+: Disney round-trip transfer is about $360 before tips mindset. Two rideshares each way in the middle of the range can undercut that — or blow past it on surge. Shared shuttle often lands in between. Price the whole party, both directions, including return-day timing to your flight.

— If you’re driving

Parking: onsite vs offsite

Port Authority parking sits next to the terminals — park and walk, no shuttle. Offsite lots trade a lower daily rate for a shuttle ride on both ends.

Onsite (Port Authority) Offsite lot + shuttle
Typical rate $20 per day + tax Arrival and departure days both count Often ~$8–15/day Book ahead; read shuttle hours
Reservations Not taken — pay on arrival (credit card; no cash) Usually reserved online; confirmation email matters
Walk to terminal Short walk from garage/lot Shuttle to terminal; luggage may need an extra hop
Best for Short cruises, ease on embark & return morning Longer sailings where daily savings add up
Hidden cost Higher total on 7-night+ sailings Wait time after you’re tired on return day

Example: 4-night sailing ≈ 5 billed parking days at onsite rates (~$100 + tax before you count gas/tolls). A cheap offsite lot that makes you wait 40 minutes after debarkation is not cheap. Confirm current Port Canaveral rates on the port’s parking page before you sail — they can change.

— Night before

Where to stay near Port Canaveral

Pick the hotel zone for the morning you want — not the Instagram night before.

Closest · easiest embarkation

Cape Canaveral / port hotels

Shortest morning transfer. Ideal if your only job is sleep → coffee → pier. Less “vacation vibe,” more logistics win.

Beach night · still close

Cocoa Beach / Cocoa

Beach walk or casual dinner, ~15–25 minutes to the terminals with buffer. Popular pre-cruise night for families.

Airport convenience

MCO / Airport hotels

Sensible if you land late. Budget an early outbound transfer (Disney shuttle from select airport hotels, shared van, or rideshare).

Only if you plan for it

Disney World / Orlando parks

Fun add-on, long embarkation morning. Land+sea Disney transfer helps — leaving parks traffic for a Port Arrival Time does not.

  • Parking at the hotel. Confirm overnight + whether you can leave a car (usually no) if you’re taking a shuttle in the morning.
  • Port Arrival Time. Use the window Disney assigns you in the app. Showing up hours early often means waiting outside.
  • Breakfast. Hotel breakfast that opens early beats hunting for food with kids and luggage.
— Sail morning

Embarkation day checklist

  • Confirm terminal and Port Arrival Time in the Disney Cruise Line app the night before.
  • Have photo IDs / passports, booking confirmation, and any medical forms accessible — not buried under packing cubes.
  • If driving: gas + SunPass / toll payment ready for SR 528; credit card for parking.
  • If transferring: leave earlier than the optimistic map ETA. Midday storms and beach-weekend traffic are normal here.
  • Carry-on first day: swimsuit, meds, chargers, kids’ comfort items — luggage delivery to the room can lag.
— South Florida · A

PortMiami

Disney’s Miami homeport for select Caribbean and Bahamas itineraries. The port sits on Dodge Island — short hop from downtown once you’re past airport or causeway traffic. Confirm your terminal in the Disney Cruise Line app; assignments can shift.

Primary airportMIA
MIA drive15–30+ min
Onsite parking~$20–35/day
Alt airportFLL ~35–55 min
— Miami airports

MIA vs FLL for PortMiami

Airport Drive to port Best for Watch-outs
Miami (MIA) ~15–30 min Shortest transfer; most straightforward Disney start from Miami Terminal sprawl + baggage claim time; afternoon storms and I-95 / airport connectors can stretch the ride
Fort Lauderdale (FLL) ~35–55+ min Sometimes cheaper flights; useful if you’re already in Broward I-95 congestion is real — don’t bank on an optimistic map ETA for a morning Port Arrival Time

By car: Port Boulevard tunnel from I-395 (toll-free into the port complex) or MacArthur Causeway from South Beach. Follow terminal signs once you’re on Dodge Island.

— How to choose

Compare transfer options the same way every time

  • All-in cost. Ticket/ride price + tip + luggage fees + kid seats if needed.
  • Time tax. Shared shuttles often wait for multiple flights; private cars leave when you leave.
  • Stress tax. Disney’s transfer costs more but removes decision-making on embarkation morning.
  • Return day. Debark timing + airport security reality matters as much as the outbound ride. A cheap lot or slow shared van hurts more when you’re flying home the same day.
