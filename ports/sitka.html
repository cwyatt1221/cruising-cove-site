Port of call · Alaska

Sitka.

Historic Russian Alaska on the Outer Coast — wildlife tours, a walkable town core, and a different feel from Juneau’s glacier-and-tram day.

— The day

Pick one job for the day

Sitka shines as wildlife / fort / ocean, historic town walk, or one longer nature outing. Confirm tender vs pier status in Navigator — tender lines change the math.

Wildlife

Ocean wildlife tour

Whales, otters, and seabirds are the draw — motion remedies help sensitive guests.

History

Town + Russian Alaska sites

Compact historic core with churches, parks, and shoreline walks.

Nature

Raptors / trails / fortress

One nature or fortress outing — don’t stack it with a long boat afterward if time is tight.

— Disney vs independent

What to book, what to skip

  • Book Disney when: wildlife boats need timing guarantees and tender logistics feel stressful.
  • Go independent when: you’re doing a town walk with a clear tender buffer.
  • Skip: long boat + long trail + museum deep-dive in one call.
  • Skip: underpacking rain layers — Sitka can be wet.

— Food

What to eat ashore

  • Downtown. Casual seafood and cafés for a sit-down break.
  • Boat days. Pack snacks; confirm inclusions.
  • Warm layers + warm drinks beat fashion-only packing.
— Shopping

What’s worth bringing home

  • Town shops. Alaska art and souvenirs — set a budget.
  • Shop after your main outing so tender lines don’t sneak up.
— Safety

With kids in daylight

  • Rain-ready shoes with grip.
  • Wildlife tours: follow crew instructions.
  • Tender platforms can be slippery — hold kids’ hands.
— Timing

Work backward from all-aboard

  • Add tender wait to every plan.
  • Wildlife boats: hard boarding times.
  • Town walk is the best weather backup.
— Priority

The one thing repeats push

Get on the water if wildlife is why you booked Sitka. The historic town is lovely — the Outer Coast wildlife is the differentiator.
