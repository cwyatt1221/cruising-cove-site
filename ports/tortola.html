— The day

Pick one job for the day

Tortola is best as beach club, sail / snorkel, or Road Town + viewpoint — not a full island drive with every stop. Confirm tender vs pier status in Navigator for your sailing.

Road Town + lookout

Shorter transfer day with shopping and a viewpoint — good with little kids.

— Disney vs independent

What to book, what to skip

  • Book Disney when: the beach or sail is farther out and you want the ship-wait cushion.
  • Go independent when: you’re keeping the day close to Road Town with a clear taxi plan.
  • Skip: sail + far beach + shopping marathon.
  • Skip: underpacking motion remedies for sail days if anyone gets queasy.

— Food

What to eat ashore

  • Beach clubs. Lunch packages keep the day simple.
  • Road Town. Casual spots for a sit-down if you’re not beach-bound.
  • Boat days. Confirm what’s included vs bring snacks for kids.
— Shopping

What’s worth bringing home

  • Road Town. Souvenirs and light duty-free — set a budget.
  • Pier retail. Convenience priced for cruise traffic.
— Safety

With kids in daylight

  • Stick to known clubs and booked boats.
  • Sun, reef footwear, and hydration matter.
  • USD makes payments simple — still carry small bills for taxis.
— Timing

Work backward from all-aboard

  • Beach transfers need padding on winding roads.
  • Sail days: be back in the tender/pier line early.
  • One job beats a rushed island circuit.
— Priority

The one thing repeats push

Get on the water or on a real beach. Tortola’s memory is usually blue water — not another pier shop loop.
