Naples.

A Mediterranean call with big choices — Pompeii, Amalfi Coast views, or a Naples food day. Pick one job; the roads and crowds will enforce it.

— The day

Pick one job for the day

Naples days fail when families try Pompeii and Amalfi together. Choose Pompeii / ruins, Amalfi Coast viewpoint day, or Naples pizza + old city. Confirm pier logistics and all-aboard in Navigator.

Signature sight

Pompeii

Walkable ruins with serious sun exposure — good shoes, water, and a guided option help.

Scenic

Amalfi Coast

Views and towns along winding roads — beautiful and transfer-heavy; not a casual add-on.

Low transfer

Naples food + stroll

Pizza, a paced old-city walk, and less van time — underrated with kids.

— Disney vs independent

What to book, what to skip

  • Book Disney when: Pompeii or Amalfi needs organized transport and return timing.
  • Go independent when: you’re staying in Naples proper with a clear taxi plan.
  • Skip: Pompeii + Amalfi in one call.
  • Skip: ruins without shade/water plans in summer.
— Transportation

How far is everything?

Destination From cruise pier How most families go Notes
Naples historic center Short–moderate Taxi or walk/taxi combo Closest “city day” option
Pompeii ~30–60+ min each way Tour, train, or taxi Pad for lines and heat fatigue
Amalfi Coast towns Longer, winding Disney or vetted tour Highest traffic / motion-sickness variable
  • Roads. Amalfi routes are scenic and slow — buffer is everything.
  • Disney transfer. Strong for Pompeii and Coast days. See Port Adventures.
— Food

What to eat ashore

  • Pizza. Naples is the pizza day — do it on a city-focused call.
  • Tour days. Confirm whether lunch is included; pack snacks for ruins.
  • Hydration. Non-negotiable at Pompeii in summer.
— Shopping

What’s worth bringing home

  • Naples center. Souvenirs and food gifts — keep bags zipped in crowds.
  • Don’t shop away your Pompeii return window.
— Safety

With kids in daylight

  • Crowded tourist zones: zipped bags, awareness.
  • Ruins: uneven stones, sun, and tired kids — slow down.
  • Coast roads: motion sickness remedies for sensitive passengers.
— Timing

Work backward from all-aboard

  • One outing only — Pompeii or Amalfi or Naples.
  • Leave sites earlier than you think.
  • City pizza day is the best backup if tours cancel.
— Priority

The one thing repeats push

Commit to Pompeii or commit to pizza-and-paseo — not a diluted version of both. Naples rewards decisive plans.
