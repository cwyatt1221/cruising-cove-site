Roatán.

Honduras’ Bay Island stop — reefs, West Bay beaches, and zip-line days, usually with a transfer from the cruise pier.

— The day

Pick one job for the day

Roatán rewards beach + snorkel or one adventure outing — not both at marathon pace. Confirm which pier you’re on and how long the ride is to West Bay before you book anything.

Most families

West Bay beach day

Clear water, chairs, and snorkel from shore or a short boat — the classic Roatán call.

In the water

Reef / snorkel boat

Book age-appropriate trips; reef conditions vary — listen to guides.

Adventure

Zip line / canopy

One paid adrenaline outing with a timed return — skip stacking a far beach afterward.

— Disney vs independent

What to book, what to skip

  • Book Disney when: transfers are long, you’re with little kids, or you want the ship-wait cushion.
  • Go independent when: you have a clear taxi/shuttle plan to a known West Bay club and a real buffer.
  • Skip: zip line + distant beach + shopping marathon.
  • Skip: assuming the pier drops you on West Bay sand — many calls need a van ride.

— Food

What to eat ashore

  • Beach clubs. Lunch packages keep sandy kids fed without a restaurant hunt.
  • Near pier. Fine for a quick bite if you’re not beach-bound.
  • Water. Hydrate aggressively — island sun is intense.
— Shopping

What’s worth bringing home

  • Pier / craft stalls. Souvenirs and wood crafts — set a budget.
  • Pressure pitches. Decline calmly and keep moving toward your booked plan.
— Safety

With kids in daylight

  • Stick to known beach clubs and booked operators for boats and zip lines.
  • Reef: follow guide instructions; don’t freestyle far from the group.
  • Sun and current awareness matter more than crime for most tourist beach days.
— Timing

Work backward from all-aboard

  • West Bay transfers need padding — traffic and shared vans add time.
  • Be in the return vehicle earlier than you think.
  • Have a pier backup if weather cancels boat days.
— Priority

The one thing repeats push

Get in the water at West Bay (or a comparable clear beach). That’s the Roatán day most families remember — not another pier shop loop.
