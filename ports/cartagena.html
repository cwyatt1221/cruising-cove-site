Port of call · Colombia

Cartagena.

Walled old city, serious heat, and a pier that usually needs a transfer — a signature stop on Disney Magic Panama Canal sailings.

— The day

One historic core, lots of heat

Cartagena’s cruise day is almost always about the Ciudad Amurallada (walled old city) — plazas, colorful streets, fort views, and lunch in the shade. Pier-to-old-city is typically a transfer, not a stroll. Plan water, shade, and a hard return time before you chase every balcony photo.

Signature sight

Walled old city

Clock tower gate, plazas, and walkable colonial streets — the reason families book this call.

Viewpoint

Castle / walls

San Felipe or wall walks for views — choose one paid climb, not every fort on the map.

Low stress

Guided old-city walk

A short Disney or independent walking tour can cut decision fatigue in the heat.

— Disney vs independent

What to book, what to skip

  • Book Disney when: you want transfer + guided old city with the ship-wait cushion, or it’s your first time in Cartagena with kids.
  • Go independent when: you’re comfortable with pier taxis/shuttles, staying in the tourist old-city core, and holding a conservative return buffer.
  • Skip: far beach days that burn transfer time on a city-focused call.
  • Skip: midday marathon walking with no shade or lunch plan — heat ends days early.

  • Taxis. Use clearly marked tourist/taxi options; agree fare or use a meter/app approach your agent recommends; keep small bills.
  • Disney transfer. Strong default for first-timers who want logistics handled. See Port Adventures filters.
— Food

What to eat ashore

  • Old city. Sit-down lunch in shade beats grazing in the noon sun. Tourist plazas have plenty of options.
  • Hydration. Treat water like a packing item — heat sneaks up fast with kids.
  • Street smart. Busy restaurants with turnover are the usual short-cruise approach.
— Shopping

What’s worth bringing home

  • Old city shops. Emeralds, crafts, and souvenirs — set a budget; quality and authenticity vary widely.
  • Pressure pitches. Decline timeshare and aggressive street sales calmly and keep walking.
— Safety

With kids in daylight

  • Stay in the main tourist old-city corridors in daylight; keep valuables zipped and low-profile.
  • Heat exhaustion is more common than “adventure gone wrong.” Shade, water, slow pace.
  • Use booked operators or well-marked taxis for transfers — don’t freestyle far outside the tourist core.
  • Carry a backup payment method; have some local cash or widely accepted small USD as your plan requires.
— Timing

Work backward from all-aboard

  • Transfer time is the schedule. Leave the old city earlier than a pier-adjacent Caribbean call.
  • Midday heat will slow kids — plan a sit-down break, not continuous walking.
  • One fort or a deep plaza wander — not both at marathon pace.
— Priority

The one thing repeats push

Get inside the walls and walk the plazas slowly. Cartagena’s magic is the old city atmosphere — not racing every attraction. Protect transfer buffer like it’s part of the excursion.
