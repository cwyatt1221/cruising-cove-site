Free, dollar by dollar

Real ways to save before you ever swipe a card onboard.

Stack the Target discount

Disney Gift Cards bought with a Target Circle Card (formerly RedCard) get an automatic 5% off — and Disney accepts gift cards toward your deposit, final payment, and onboard spending.

Six months, no interest

Book directly through Disney Cruise Line and pay with a Chase Disney Visa or Disney Premier Visa, and the balance qualifies for 0% promotional APR for six months from the purchase date — a perk of the Disney-branded cards specifically, not Chase cards in general.

Check the warehouse clubs

Sam's Club and BJ's Wholesale periodically sell Disney Gift Cards below face value, sometimes on top of the Target discount — worth a look before a big payment comes due.

Card terms and retailer discounts change often — we'll keep this page current, and it's always worth confirming the offer at checkout.
