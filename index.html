— The idea

Why this exists

Hi! I'm Cassondra, and I want to help you make the most of your Disney cruise. I've spent enough hours in Disney Cruise Line planning groups to notice the same questions surface on every sailing thread — which stateroom actually has the noise problem, whether an excursion is worth booking independently, what a cruise really costs once gratuities and Palo and excursions are added in. Disney's own materials won't tell you any of that. The internet has answers, but they're scattered across forums, Facebook groups, and a dozen blogs.

Cruising Cove is an attempt to put that knowledge in one place — structured, searchable, and honest about the parts of planning that are genuinely stressful. It's being built in public, a page at a time, starting now.
