— Included · main event

Rotational dining

Your cruise fare includes main dining. Each night you go to a different themed restaurant in a small set (usually three on a short cruise, more variety on longer ones), but you keep the same servers and dining time. The restaurant changes; your team doesn’t — that’s the Disney difference most families notice by night two.

Piece How it works Plan for
Seating time First / early or second / late seating (exact times vary by ship and sailing) Match kids’ sleep windows — late seating can collide with shows and bedtime
Tablemates You may share a larger table with other guests unless your party fills it Request a private table in advance or at Guest Services if that matters
Character dining Some rotational nights include character visits in specific restaurants Check Navigator for “which night / which room” on your sailing
Dress Smart casual most nights; Pirate Night / formal nights elevate Pack one nicer outfit if you care about photos
— Tip

If your seating fights the showtimes you want, ask Guest Services early — adjustments happen, but popular slots go first. Also: kids’ clubs often coordinate pickup around dining times so parents can still do an adult show or specialty dinner.

— Extra · adults 18+

Adult specialty dining

Reservation-only restaurants with a cover charge. Not included in the cruise fare. These are the “best meal of the trip” venues for a lot of couples — and they fill as soon as booking windows open.

Venue type Typical ships Rough cover (pp) Best for
Palo / Palo Steakhouse Classic Palo on Dream-class & older; Palo Steakhouse on Wish-class ~$50–55 Brunch or dinner · confirm current Italian / steakhouse celebration without Remy’s fine-dining formality
Remy Dream & Fantasy Higher prix-fixe + gratuity Special-occasion French dining
Enchanté Wish-class Higher prix-fixe Adult exclusive Wish-class fine dining counterpart to Remy
  • When to book. As soon as your Castaway Club / guest booking window opens. Port days and “quiet” nights go first for many people — Pirate Night and sea days are popular too.
  • Kids. These venues are 18+. Plan a club night or a trusted dinner plan for younger guests.
  • Gratuity. Specialty bills add gratuity separately from your prepaid stateroom tips. Fold it into the true cost picture.
— Included · flexible

Buffet, quick service & soft-serve

You are never required to sit a full rotational dinner. Buffet / marketplace venues (names vary by ship — Cabanas on many ships, ship-specific cafés on Wish-class) cover breakfast and lunch with wide choice. Pool-deck quick service covers burgers, pizza, and rotating bites most of the day. Soft-serve is free and legitimately a kid highlight.

  • Port mornings. Early buffet or room-service continental beats rushing a sit-down meal before a transfer.
  • Skip rotational? Allowed. Tell your servers if you’ll miss a night so they’re not holding a table unnecessarily — courtesy matters on a small dining team.
  • Hours. Quick service is not always 24/7. Check Navigator for “still open” before you promise a midnight pizza run.
— Included with caveats

Room service

Available around the clock in most staterooms. A basic continental breakfast is typically complimentary; many other items carry a delivery charge or suggested gratuity. Order by phone or the Disney Cruise Line Navigator app.

  • Use it for early port mornings, post-show hunger, or a toddler who will only eat in pajamas.
  • Verify the current complimentary vs charged list in the app for your sailing — policies get tweaked.
— Families

Kids menus & dietary needs

Kids

Kids’ menus

Every rotational restaurant has a children’s menu. Servers learn preferences fast — use that. Soft-serve and pool-deck options cover picky mid-days.

Allergies

Dietary requests

Submit allergen and specialty requests through Disney before sailing, then reconfirm with the head server on night one. They can plan ahead for the whole rotation.

Adults + kids

Split evenings

Specialty dinner + kids in Oceaneer’s Club / Club or with a sitter plan is a common anniversary play. Book clubs and Palo in the same planning session.

Budget

What’s already paid

Rotational, buffet, most quick service, and soft-serve are in the fare. Alcohol, specialty cover charges, and many room-service extras are not.
