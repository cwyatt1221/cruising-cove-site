Dining Onboard

How eating actually works on a Disney cruise.

Four different ways to eat onboard, each with its own rhythm — here's what to expect from each.

Rotational Dining

Your included main dining — you rotate through a small set of themed restaurants over the course of the cruise, keeping the same servers and dining time each night while your tablemates and menu change.

  • Assigned at booking, but requests can often be adjusted onboard
  • Character dining rotates through some of these rooms on certain nights
  • Included in your cruise fare — no extra charge

Adult Dining — Palo & Remy

Reservation-only, adults-only venues with a cover charge, generally considered the best food on the ship. These book up fast — reserving as early as your booking window allows is worth it.

  • Separate cover charge, not included in your cruise fare
  • Adults-only (18+)
  • Reservations open before your sail date and fill quickly

Snacks & Quick Service

Casual, no-reservation food available most of the day near the pool deck — burgers, pizza, and rotating quick options, plus soft-serve that's genuinely a highlight for a lot of families.

  • Included in your fare, no reservation needed
  • Hours vary — generally not 24/7
  • Good option on port days when the main dining rooms are quieter

Room Service

Available around the clock in your stateroom. A basic continental breakfast is complimentary; most other room service items carry a small charge or gratuity.

  • Available 24 hours
  • Order by phone or through the Navigator app
  • Handy for early port mornings or a tired kid who wants to eat in pajamas
