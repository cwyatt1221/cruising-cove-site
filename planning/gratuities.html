Onboard spending

Gratuities, without the surprise.

Recommended daily rates, automatic add to your folio, how to adjust at Guest Services, and which tips are separate.

Last updated: August 4, 2026

— Recommended amounts

Per guest, per night

Standard stateroom$16.00per guest / night
Concierge$27.25per guest / night
Applies toAll agesinfants included

Official Disney guidance (confirm before you sail): standard recommended gratuity is $16.00 USD per night, per stateroom guest, including infants and children. Concierge staterooms and suites are $27.25 per night, per guest, covering the extra Concierge host/lounge support as well as dining and stateroom teams.

Prepaid optional gratuities are distributed among dining room server, assistant server, head server, and stateroom host (plus Concierge roles when applicable).

— How it posts

Auto-add, prepay, or adjust

  • Default: if you don’t prepay, Disney adds the recommended amount to your onboard account.
  • Prepay: via My Reservations online (or ask your travel agent) for a carefree folio.
  • Adjust onboard: visit Guest Services if you want to change or remove the optional amount.
— Separate tips

Not covered by the daily amount

  • Adult / premium dining (Palo, Remy, Enchanté, etc.)
  • Bar, beverage, wine, and deck service tabs
  • Spa & salon
  • Room service, Port Adventures, and most shop / poolside extras

Disney currently notes an 18% gratuity added on bar, beverage, wine, adult/premium dining, deck service, and spa services — charged to your onboard account. Room service and some venues may still invite an additional discretionary tip; follow the check and Guest Services guidance on your sailing.
