— Grab list

What to book first when the window opens

  1. Cabanas at Castaway Cay or Lookout Cay — if you want one, treat this as minute-one.
  2. High-demand Port Adventures (Atlantis, Stingray City boats, glacier flights, White Pass train).
  3. Specialty dining — Palo / Palo Steakhouse, Remy, Enchanté; brunch slots go fast on short sailings.
  4. Spa / adult spa day if that’s your priority — after the three above for most families.
