Loyalty program

Castaway Club, decoded.

Disney Cruise Line’s free loyalty program — Silver through Pearl — based on completed sailings. Earlier booking windows are the perk most families feel first.

Last updated: July 30, 2026

— How it works

Automatic after your first eligible cruise

Finish one eligible Disney Cruise Line sailing and you’re in Castaway Club — no signup fee. Levels are based on completed eligible cruises (each sailing counts as one, whether it’s 3 nights or 14). Some fare types (media, vendor, certain Cast Member business rates) don’t count.

  • Benefits follow the stateroom. Everyone in your cabin gets the perks of the highest-level Castaway Club member in that room.
  • Stay active. Members generally need to book or sail at least once every five years to remain in the program.
  • Concierge ≠ Castaway Club. Concierge is a stateroom category with its own early access. You can be Concierge and also Silver/Gold/Platinum/Pearl.
— The four levels

Silver, Gold, Platinum, Pearl

After 1 sailing

Silver

  • Book new itineraries ~1 day before the general public
  • Activities (Port Adventures, specialty dining, spa) ~90 days before sail
  • Online check-in / port arrival ~33 days out
  • Exclusive terminal check-in where available
  • Castaway Club member phone line + communications
  • Welcome-back-aboard gift
After 5 sailings

Gold

  • Everything in Silver
  • New itineraries ~2 days early
  • Activities ~105 days before sail
  • Online check-in ~35 days out
  • Private onboard reception on sailings of 8 nights or longer
  • Select member discounts
After 10 sailings

Platinum

  • Everything in Gold
  • New itineraries ~3 days early
  • Activities ~120 days before sail
  • Online check-in ~38 days out
  • Complimentary dinner at Palo / Palo Steakhouse / Palo Trattoria (rules apply)
After 25 sailings

Pearl

  • Everything in Platinum
  • New itineraries ~4 days early
  • Activities ~123 days before sail
  • Automatic Boarding Group 2 assignment
  • Complimentary Unlimited Digital Photo Package (stateroom of the Pearl member)
  • Pearl is not a first-time status — it’s the top Castaway Club tier

Benefit details and windows can change. Confirm in your MyDisney / Disney Cruise Line account and the official Castaway Club FAQs before you plan around a perk.

— Planning tips

How to use your tier

  • Know your open day. Put the activity window on a calendar and be logged into My Cruise Activities at midnight Eastern when it opens if you want cabanas or top excursions.
  • Link staterooms carefully. Benefits don’t automatically spill to a separate cabin — only the highest member in that room sets the window for that cabin’s bookings.
  • Platinum / Pearl Palo. Complimentary specialty dinner has age and sailing rules — confirm eligibility for your voyage before you count on it.
  • First cruise? You’re not Castaway Club until you sail. Plan around the ~75-day first-timer activity window, then you’ll unlock Silver for the next trip.
