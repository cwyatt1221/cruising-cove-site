Policy update

What changed in June 2026.

Doors, alcohol, and selfie sticks — the three rules that rolled out together for sailings starting June 3, 2026.

Last updated: August 4, 2026

— Effective date

Sailings from June 3, 2026

Disney updated several guest policies in late May 2026. They apply fleet-wide as each ship’s sailings depart on or after June 3, 2026. Confirm the live wording on Disney’s prohibited-items and FAQ pages before you pack.

— 1

Stateroom door decorations

Decorations stay on your door only — not hallway walls or ceilings. Magnets preferred; no tape/adhesives/hooks that damage the door. Damage fee can run about $100 per incident. Fish Extenders for gift exchanges remain a normal tradition.

Full door decoration guide

— 2

Carry-on alcohol

Guests 21+ may bring one unopened wine/champagne (≤750 ml) or six beers (≤12 oz) — not both — in carry-on at embarkation only. Round-trip Singapore (Adventure) sailings: 18+ may use the same allowance. Port purchases are held until the end of the cruise. Corkage in dining rooms is typically about $20/bottle.

Alcohol FAQ

— 3

Selfie sticks & tripods

Selfie sticks, hand-held extension poles, and tripods may be used in most areas onboard if retracted and under 18 inches. Longer gear can come onboard but must stay in the stateroom and is for shore use only.
