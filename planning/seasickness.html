Feeling steady

Seasickness and motion.

Most guests feel fine — here’s how to stack the odds if you don’t.

Last updated: August 4, 2026

— Reality check

How rough is a Disney ship?

Modern Disney ships have stabilizers that cut a lot of side-to-side roll. Caribbean and Bahamas itineraries are usually gentle. Open-ocean days (Transatlantic, some Hawaii segments, Panama Canal repositioning sailings, stormy Alaska crossings) feel different — plan remedies before you need them.

— Stateroom choice

Midship and lower decks

  • Lower + midship generally moves less than high/forward/aft verandahs.
  • Use each ship’s stateroom guides and deck plans when motion sensitivity is a hard constraint.
  • A verandah can help psychologically (horizon view) even if motion is similar to an oceanview next door.
— Remedies

What families actually use

  • Over-the-counter motion meds (follow dosing; some cause drowsiness)
  • Ginger candy, acupressure bands — mixed results, low downside
  • Fresh air midship on an open deck; eyes on the horizon
  • Avoid heavy reading/phone scrolling in your bunk during a swell
  • Ship’s medical center can help if home remedies aren’t enough — fees apply
— Kids

Pack a plan before whale-watching boats or tender ports — small boats feel worse than the cruise ship. See port pages for tender notes (e.g. Grand Cayman).
