Last morning

Disembarkation day, step by step.

Luggage tags, Express Walk-Off, Folio, breakfast, and when to book flights — so the last morning feels orderly instead of rushed.

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Embarkation day Departure ports
— Timing reality check

When the ship clears

  • Docking is often around 6:00 AM on the final morning (weather and ports can shift this).
  • Disembarkation commonly starts around 7:30 AM, with Disney aiming to have guests off by roughly 9:00 AM when things run on time.
  • Flights: for most home ports, skip anything earlier than about 11:30 AM unless you have Express Walk-Off, live near the airport, and are comfortable with risk. Confirm the guidance Disney posts for your sailing.

— Night before

Set yourself up before you sleep

  • Check your Folio in Navigator (charges, Wi‑Fi, photos, spa). Dispute anything odd before you leave the ship.
  • Confirm gratuities — automatic service charges and any adjustments. Overview: gratuities guide.
  • Choose your exit style in Navigator when Disney offers it:
    • Regular / assisted luggage — place tagged bags in the corridor by the posted time; you’ll claim them in the terminal after leaving the ship.
    • Express Walk-Off (self-assist) — you keep all luggage with you overnight and walk off with it in the morning. Faster if you can roll everything and don’t mind living out of bags.
  • Attach Disney luggage tags to every bag you’re sending ashore. Keep one soft bag / backpack as your overnight carry.
  • Overnight carry must include: meds, passports/IDs, phones + chargers, jewelry/valuables, tickets/boarding passes, a change of clothes if you need it, and anything you’d be stuck without if a tagged bag is delayed.
  • Kids’ comfort items stay with you — don’t put the only pacifier or favorite stuffed animal in a tagged bag.
  • Port alcohol or held purchases — if Disney held bottles from shore days, collect them when instructed (often with luggage flow).
  • Pack decorations / Fish Extenders the night before if you’re not Express Walk-Off; leave the corridor clean for stewards.
— Express Walk-Off tip

If you pick Express Walk-Off, treat the last night like a hotel checkout: bags packed, carry-ons ready by the door, and nothing left hanging on the stateroom door that you care about keeping.

— Morning of

Breakfast → gangway → bags

  1. Wake with a buffer — docking announcements, elevator crowds, and last-minute Folio checks take longer than you think.
  2. Breakfast — usually open early in main venues. Eat before your group is called if kids melt down when hungry.
  3. Kids clubs are typically closed or limited on disembarkation morning — don’t plan a last Oceaneer drop-off.
  4. Listen for your group / watch Navigator — walk-off is staggered. Don’t line up an hour early blocking corridors.
  5. Key to the World cards stay with you until you’re fully off (you may still need them for Folio or photos).
  6. Customs / immigration — have passports or accepted IDs ready; follow crew directions for your home port.
  7. Claim tagged luggage in the terminal (unless Express Walk-Off). Tip porters if they help you to rideshare / shuttle / parking.
  8. Ground transport — Disney transfers, rideshare, rental cars, and port parking each have different post-cruise waits. Compare: departure ports guide.
— After you leave

Airport, parking, and open-jaw sailings

  • Same-day flights: build in port exit + bag claim + traffic + airport security. Early domestic flights are the #1 last-morning stressor.
  • Port parking: know whether you prepay, keep the ticket with you, and how long the walk/shuttle is after debarkation.
  • Open-jaw / repositioning cruises (different embark and debark cities) — confirm the debark airport, not the one you flew into on day one. Example notes: Panama Canal sailings.
  • Souvenir overflow — popcorn buckets and shop finds are bulky; leave soft-bag space or ship items home earlier if you’re already at weight limits.
— Quick list

Printable last-morning checklist

  • ☐ Folio reviewed; surprises resolved
  • ☐ Gratuities confirmed
  • ☐ Luggage tags on bags or Express Walk-Off packed and with you
  • ☐ Overnight carry: IDs, meds, chargers, valuables
  • ☐ Flight / transfer time still realistic
  • ☐ Kids’ must-haves not in tagged luggage
  • ☐ Door décor / extenders packed
  • ☐ Customs documents ready
  • ☐ Ground transport plan (transfer, rideshare pin, parking ticket)
