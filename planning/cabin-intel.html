Category isn’t the whole story

Inside vs verandah matters — but so does whether your door sits on an elevator lobby, under the pool deck, or behind a lifeboat. Cruising Cove’s thin cabin intel is curated planning notes (v1), not a live cabin database. Always verify the specific stateroom in Disney’s deck plans before you pay.
