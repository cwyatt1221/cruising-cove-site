Youth clubs

Kids clubs by age.

Oceaneer, Edge, Vibe, and the nursery — who goes where, what’s free, and what to register on embarkation day.

— By ship class

What changes ship to ship

  • Wish / Treasure / Destiny: Oceaneer spaces themed around Marvel, Star Wars, Mickey, and more — same age bands, different rooms. Destiny details: Destiny clubs.
  • Dream / Fantasy: Classic Oceaneer Club + Lab split; Edge and Vibe midship/aft depending on deck plans.
  • Magic / Wonder: Smaller ships, same age structure; Wonder also hosts Bluey programming on select sailings.
  • Adventure (Singapore): Youth clubs follow Disney’s age model inside Adventure’s themed lands — verify room names and hours in Navigator; don’t assume Wish-class maps.
— Parent tips

Registration and expectations

  • Register Oceaneer on embarkation day — it’s one of the highest-ROI lines to stand in early.
  • Nursery is not included; book peak dinner and spa hours as soon as your Castaway Club activity window allows.
  • Toddlers on Destiny: see toddler tips.
  • Club hours shrink on port days; sea days run longer. Always trust Navigator over memory from a prior cruise.
