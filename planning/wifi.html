Staying connected

Wi-Fi packages, compared.

What’s free, what you pay for, and who actually needs streaming at sea.

Last updated: August 4, 2026

— Included

Free basic connectivity

Basic connectivity for iMessage, WhatsApp, and the Disney Cruise Line Navigator app is free. That covers a lot of family coordination without buying a package.

— Paid tiers

Internet vs internet + streaming

Tier Best for Ballpark (per device / day)
Free messaging / Navigator Texts via iMessage/WhatsApp, schedules, muster info $0
Internet Email, light browsing, social posts, work Slack in a pinch ~$30
Internet + streaming Video calls, Netflix/Disney+, heavier uploads ~$49

Prices move by sailing and promotion — treat the dollars as planning midpoints and confirm in the Disney Cruise Line app for your reservation. Packages are typically sold per device.

— Who needs what

Practical recommendations

  • Most families: free tier + one internet device for the adult who handles bookings/photos.
  • Work-from-ship / heavy uploaders: internet on the laptop; don’t assume streaming speeds equal home fiber.
  • Teens who “need” TikTok: budget a device package or lean into free messaging and ship entertainment.
  • Castaway / Lookout days: packages still help for photos, but island Wi-Fi isn’t the point — enjoy the beach.
