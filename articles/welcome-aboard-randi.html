Welcome aboard Randi — our newest travel agent

Meet Randi DeCap of Salt & Compass Travel Co — a Disney Cruise specialist helping families choose the right ship, stateroom, and onboard plan with confidence.

Published September 26, 2026 · Travel agents

Randi DeCap

Salt & Compass Travel Co · Bettendorf, IA

4 years specializing · 30 Disney sailings planned · sailed Treasure, Wish, Destiny, and Fantasy

Randi helps families plan Disney cruises with confidence — from choosing the right ship and stateroom to navigating dining, onboard experiences, and everything in between. You’ll have an experienced Disney Cruise Line specialist in your corner from the day you book until you sail.

About Randi

Hi, I’m Randi, owner of Salt & Compass Travel Co. and a Disney Cruise Line specialist who genuinely loves helping families experience everything that makes a Disney cruise so special. I’ve sailed Disney Cruise Line myself, including Concierge, and I bring that firsthand experience into every vacation I plan.

Whether you’re trying to choose the right ship and itinerary, wondering which stateroom is best for your family, or have absolutely no idea what to expect on your first Disney cruise, I’ll walk you through it all. My goal is to take the overwhelm out of planning while making sure you don’t miss the details and experiences that matter most to your family.

She’s planned 30 Disney sailings, sailed six times herself aboard Treasure, Wish, Destiny, and Fantasy, and works from Bettendorf, Iowa. From the day you choose your cruise until it’s time to step onboard, you’ll have someone in your corner who knows Disney Cruise Line and cares about the details as much as you do.

Specialties

  • First-timers
  • Wish-class & Dream-class ships
  • Family groups
  • Adult sailings
  • Castaway Cay & Lookout Cay
  • Concierge

What you can expect

  • Personalized cruise recommendations based on your family, not a one-size-fits-all suggestion
  • Guidance choosing the right ship, itinerary, stateroom, and location onboard
  • Honest advice about what’s worth the splurge and where you can save
  • Price monitoring and eligible discounts applied when available
  • Help navigating important dates, reservations, and pre-cruise planning
  • Personalized recommendations for dining, activities, port adventures, and onboard experiences
  • Firsthand tips from an experienced Disney Cruise Line traveler
  • Detailed pre-travel information so you know what to expect before you sail

Why families choose her

Planning a Disney cruise should feel exciting, not overwhelming. Randi takes the time to get to know your family, answer the questions you didn’t even know you had, and guide you through the little details that can make a big difference. She doesn’t book the cruise and send you on your way. She’s there from the moment you start planning until you step onboard, sharing firsthand experience, honest recommendations, and personalized guidance along the way.

Guests pay the same cruise fare as booking direct. Authorized agents are paid a commission by Disney Cruise Line. Randi does not charge a planning fee.
