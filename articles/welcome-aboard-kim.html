Welcome aboard Kim — our newest travel agent

Meet Kim Fanning of Magical World Vacations — a Disney Cruise specialist creating personalized, stress-free sailings for families, including neurodivergent travelers.

Published August 5, 2026 · Travel agents

Kim Fanning

Magical World Vacations · Jackson, NJ

EarMarked · Featured · 8 years specializing · 30+ Disney sailings planned

Personalized Disney and cruise vacations that make travel memorable and stress-free — with a passion for helping neurodivergent travelers and families plan trips that fit their unique needs.

About Kim

Kim is a travel advisor specializing in Disney destinations, Universal destinations, cruises, and family vacations. As a pediatric occupational therapist and the parent of autistic and ADHD children, she brings both professional expertise and lived experience to planning vacations that are accessible, inclusive, and tailored to neurodivergent travelers and their families.

She’s planned 30+ Disney sailings, personally sailed about 8, and has been aboard Magic, Wonder, Fantasy, Dream, and Wish. Based in Jackson, NJ, she’s committed to helping families navigate every detail with confidence so they can focus on making lifelong memories together.

Specialties

  • First-timers
  • Family groups & large groups
  • Adult sailings
  • Castaway Cay & Lookout Cay
  • Alaska
  • Budget planning
  • Accessibility
  • Autism & special needs

What you can expect

  • Personalized quotes that are always free
  • Reminder emails for important dates
  • Tips and tricks emails
  • Quick and personalized responses to emails and questions
  • Assistance anytime needed throughout the process or while on your trip

Why families choose her

Families choose Kim because she understands that no two vacations — or families — are alike. As both a pediatric occupational therapist and the parent of autistic and ADHD children, she knows firsthand how important thoughtful planning can be, especially when traveling with young children or neurodivergent family members. She helps families navigate the details, reduce the stress of planning, and build itineraries that match their interests, needs, and pace.

Guests pay the same cruise fare as booking direct. Authorized agents are paid a commission by Disney Cruise Line. Kim offers free concierge-level service.
