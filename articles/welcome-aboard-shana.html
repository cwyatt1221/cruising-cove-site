About Shana

Shana is the #1 Disney Cruise Line advisor at Friend Like Me Travel Co., with over $3 million in Disney Cruise Line sales and more than 800 cruises booked. She also manages a Disney Cruise planning community of over 19,000 members, where she shares firsthand tips and helps families plan daily.

As a Platinum Castaway Club member who sails frequently with her own family, Shana offers firsthand knowledge of Disney ships, itineraries, staterooms, dining, activities, excursions, and private destinations. She’s sailed Destiny, Treasure, Wish, Dream, Fantasy, Wonder, and Magic.

As a mom of an autistic child and someone with celiac disease, she understands sensory needs, routines, accessibility considerations, and gluten-free dining. She provides personalized support from booking through sailing, with complimentary planning services and onboard credit available on qualifying new bookings.

Why families choose her

Shana combines extensive booking experience with firsthand knowledge from sailing Disney Cruise Line frequently with her own family.
