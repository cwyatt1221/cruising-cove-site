Welcome aboard Rebekah — our newest travel agent

Meet Rebekah Lukins of Best Day Ever with Bek — a Disney Cruise specialist helping families with young kids plan sailings they’ll actually remember.

Published July 30, 2026 · Travel agents

Rebekah Lukins

Best Day Ever with Bek · Chicago, IL

EarMarked · 5 years specializing · 300+ Disney sailings planned

A Disney Cruise (and Diet Coke) obsessed planner who specializes in cruises for families with young kids, Disney private islands, and Alaskan adventures — plus getting you counting down to your next crispy Disney Diet Coke (or beverage of choice) onboard.

About Rebekah

Rebekah is a Disney World kid turned Disney Cruise adult. She’s been a top-3 seller of Disney Cruises at Best Day Ever Vacations for the last three years and has helped families plan over $2 million in Disney Cruises. She sails 3–5 times a year with her Disney-loving husband and toddlers, and she’s an expert on making vacations fun and relaxing for everyone from adults to babies. Her favorite Disney ship is the Treasure, and her favorite itinerary is Alaska with Juneau.

Specialties

  • First-timers
  • Wish-class ships
  • Family groups & large groups
  • Adult sailings
  • Castaway Cay & Lookout Cay
  • Alaska
  • Accessibility, mobility needs, accessible cabins, and seasickness support

What you can expect

  • Stateroom breakdown before booking
  • Ship comparison sheets to help decision-making
  • Large group coordination and linking
  • A full-length Disney Cruise Line vacation guide at booking
  • Help booking Royal Gathering
  • Excursion booking support if you want it
  • Step-by-step Cruise Check-In guide with pictures
  • Personalized transfer and hotel suggestions

Why families choose her

Families should choose an agent who’s an active Disney Cruise sailor and stays current on experiences and offerings — and that’s Rebekah.

Guests pay the same cruise fare as booking direct. Authorized agents are paid a commission by Disney Cruise Line.
