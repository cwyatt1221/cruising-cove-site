Welcome aboard Emily — our newest travel agent

Meet Emily Schultz of Magic Mom Travel — a Disney Cruise specialist helping families plan prepared, confident sailings, including autism- and sensory-friendly travel.

Published August 7, 2026 · Travel agents

Emily Schultz

Magic Mom Travel · Waukesha, WI (remote)

EarMarked · 10 years specializing · 400+ Disney sailings planned

Hi! Emily is a mom of two (one with special needs) who deeply understands how precious time together as a family is. As a seasoned cruiser and travel agent over the last decade, she’s here to help with what you need to know — and what you didn’t know you needed to know.

About Emily

As the owner of Magic Mom Travel LLC, Emily has been helping guests for the last decade and works diligently to have every family prepared. She’s an ASTA Verified Travel Advisor, a Certified Travel Industry Executive through The Travel Institute, and an IBCCES Certified Autism Travel Professional.

She’s planned 400+ Disney sailings, personally sailed about 12, and has been aboard Wish, Treasure, Fantasy, Destiny, Magic, and Wonder. Based remotely in Waukesha, WI, she guides guests from the first conversation through boarding — with free onboard credit and luggage loss insurance for her guests.

Specialties

  • First-timers
  • Wish-class & Dream-class ships
  • Family groups & large groups
  • Adult sailings
  • Castaway Cay & Lookout Cay
  • Alaska & Europe
  • Budget planning
  • Accessibility
  • Autism travel, sailing with small kids & sensory-friendly travel

What you can expect

  • Customized vacation proposals tailored to your family’s budget and interests, with custom stateroom selection
  • A go-to person for accurate, up-to-date answers
  • Personal recommendations and tips to help maximize your time
  • Price and promotional comparisons so you’re always paying the lowest possible cost
  • Full concierge service to plan your day-to-day
  • Group travel coordination
  • Logistical help getting from point A to B
  • Free onboard credit on qualifying bookings

Why families choose her

Families choose Emily because she plans from firsthand experience — sailing Disney Cruise Line with her own family and seeing the magic through her daughter’s eyes. As a parent and as someone who also sails adults-only, she knows how to tailor trips that fit real needs, budgets, and pace. Her goal is lasting family memories, booked with confidence.

Guests pay the same cruise fare as booking direct. Authorized agents are paid a commission by Disney Cruise Line. Emily offers free concierge-level service.
