Disney’s next ship class: what D23 actually said.

Three ships from 2029, not more Wish-class copies — sized between Wonder and Wish so Disney can reach more ports as the fleet goes global.

August 17, 2026 · Ships

D23 wasn’t only a Disney Believe artwork reveal. Disney Cruise Line also confirmed something planners have been waiting on: the three ships arriving after Believe are not more Wish-class copies. They’ll be a new class, sized on purpose between the classic ships and the Wish-class giants, with the first of them entering service from 2029.

Names, dining, shows, and itineraries are still TBA. Here’s what Disney actually said — and what it means if you’re deciding which ship to book in the meantime.

What Disney confirmed

  • Three ships after Disney Believe (late 2027).
  • A new class — not a fifth, sixth, and seventh Wish-class hull.
  • Size: larger than Disney Wonder (~2,713 guests) and smaller than Disney Wish (~4,000 guests).
  • First arrivals: beginning in 2029.
  • Fleet target: 13 ships by late calendar 2030 / fiscal 2031, described as new entry points for guests around the world.

Disney Experiences Chairman Thomas Mazloum told USA TODAY the smaller-than-Wish size is intentional: not every ship can call every port. As DCL expands globally, they want hulls that can reach destinations Wish-class ships can’t.

Where this sits in the fleet

Group Ships Scale (approx.)
Classic Magic, Wonder ~2,700 guests · smaller ports, Alaska, some Europe
Dream class Dream, Fantasy Mid-size · AquaDuck, Bahamas / Caribbean staples
Wish class Wish, Treasure, Destiny, Believe (2027) ~4,000 guests · Florida-heavy until Europe/West Coast deployments
Adventure Adventure (Singapore) ~6,700 guests · unique mega-ship, Asia only so far
New class (unnamed) Three ships from 2029 Between Wonder and Wish · built for more destinations

Think of it as filling the gap Magic/Wonder fans already like (more ports, less mega-ship sprawl) without giving up the newer-ship amenities families now expect.

What was not announced

  • Ship names, themes, or Imagineering artwork
  • Homeports or first itineraries
  • Dining, entertainment, or stateroom details
  • When bookings will open

Until those land, don’t hold a 2029 vacation hostage. Book the ship that exists for your dates — then watch for a homeport announcement the way people watched Destiny and Adventure.

Other D23 fleet notes (same weekend)

Useful context, not the new class itself:

  • Alaska 2027: both Magic and Wonder return.
  • Wish to Europe for the first time, with new ports of call.
  • San Diego sailings expanding (Magic and Wonder rotations through at least 2031) — a West Coast option that pairs cleanly with Disneyland trips.
  • Believe livestream: more ship detail on October 7, 2026.

How to plan around it

If you want the newest Wish-class experience before 2029, that’s Wish, Treasure, Destiny, then Believe. If you want smaller-ship Alaska or Europe, Magic and Wonder still matter — and the 2029 class is Disney saying that size of ship isn’t going away.
