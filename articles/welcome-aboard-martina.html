Welcome aboard Martina — our newest travel agent

Meet Martina Yost of Magic Mom Travel — a Disney Cruise enthusiast and mom of two who helps families choose the right sailing, stateroom, and extras without the guesswork.

Published August 9, 2026 · Travel agents

Martina Yost

Magic Mom Travel LLC · Aubrey, TX

EarMarked · 5 years specializing · 50+ Disney sailings planned

Martina is a mom of two little boys and a Disney Cruise Line enthusiast who knows what’s worth the splurge and what isn’t. She’ll help you choose the right sailing, stateroom, and extras — with personalized guidance and a little extra pixie dust along the way.

About Martina

As a travel advisor with Magic Mom Travel LLC, Martina understands how much goes into planning a family vacation — especially when you want every dollar to feel worth it. She loves helping families choose the right ship, sailing, stateroom, and extras while making the process feel easy, personal, and a little more magical.

She’s planned 50+ Disney sailings, personally sailed about 8, and has been aboard Wish, Magic, Destiny, Dream, and Fantasy. Based in Aubrey, TX, she’s especially helpful with young families, connecting staterooms, multi-generational travel, Galveston departures, and pre/post-cruise Disney stays.

Specialties

  • First-timers
  • Wish-class & Dream-class ships
  • Family groups & large groups
  • Young families & connecting staterooms
  • Multi-generational travel
  • Adult sailings
  • Castaway Cay & Lookout Cay
  • Europe
  • Budget planning
  • Galveston departures & pre/post-cruise Disney stays

What you can expect

  • Personalized stateroom and deck recommendations
  • Honest advice on where to splurge and where to save
  • Price monitoring and promotion checks after booking
  • Dining, activity & excursion booking reminders
  • Kid-age-specific planning and recommendations
  • Multi-stateroom and extended-family coordination
  • Pre-cruise check-in and documentation guidance
  • Help with hotels, flights & transportation

Why families choose her

Families choose Martina because she’s a regular mom who loves to cruise — and she’s gotten really good at knowing where to save and where it’s worth spending a little more. She doesn’t believe in cookie-cutter vacation planning. She gets to know how you travel, how you like to communicate, what your kids are like, what stresses you out, and what you value most — then uses those details to plan a trip that actually fits your family.

Guests pay the same cruise fare as booking direct. Authorized agents are paid a commission by Disney Cruise Line.
