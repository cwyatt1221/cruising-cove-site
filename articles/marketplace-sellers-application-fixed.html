Sellers: why Cruising Cove is worth a spot

This isn’t a giant open directory. It’s a Curated 10 — only ten shops on the marketplace at a time — so founding partners get real visibility with Disney cruise families who are already planning, packing, and shopping for their sailing.

  • Reach the right audience. Families come here for ships, ports, packing lists, and communities — then discover your shop while they’re in planning mode.
  • Stand out, don’t get buried. Ten exclusive listings mean your card isn’t lost in a scroll of hundreds.
  • Try it risk-free. Selected partners get a 90-day free trial, then $39/month for the first year. Cancel anytime — nothing owed if you cancel during the trial.
  • Simple to apply. Shop URL, a short directory description, and a few product photos. We review applications by hand.
