Welcome aboard Donna — our newest travel agent

Meet Donna Walters of EnchantAway Travel — a Disney Cruise specialist offering personalized planning, expert guidance, and genuine care for every family.

Published August 2, 2026 · Travel agents

Donna Walters

EnchantAway Travel · Celebration, FL

EarMarked · 2 years specializing · 25+ Disney sailings planned

Personalized planning. Expert guidance. Genuine care. Donna treats every vacation like it’s her own — because your memories matter.

About Donna

With nearly 30 years of experience caring for people as a registered nurse, Donna brings the same dedication, attention to detail, and advocacy to every vacation she plans. She takes the time to get to know you and create a trip that’s personalized to your family, budget, and travel style — no cookie-cutter format.

She’s sailed Magic, Dream, Fantasy, Wish, and Destiny, planned 25+ Disney sailings, and personally sailed about 15 herself. Based in Celebration, FL, she helps first-timers and repeat guests alike feel supported from planning through embarkation day.

Specialties

  • First-timers
  • Wish-class & Dream-class ships
  • Family groups
  • Adult sailings
  • Castaway Cay & Lookout Cay
  • Alaska & Europe
  • Budget planning
  • Accessibility & medical needs

What you can expect

  • Expert stateroom selection based on your family’s needs, budget, and preferences
  • Personalized cruise planning tailored to your travel style, celebration, and must-do experiences
  • Step-by-step guidance for the Disney Cruise Line Navigator app and online account
  • Detailed check-in and embarkation instructions before you arrive at the port
  • Assistance booking Royal Gathering reservations and guidance on other hard-to-get experiences
  • Reminders and notifications throughout the planning process
  • Support with onboard activity planning, including dining, Port Adventures, and adult-exclusive experiences
  • Private island tips for Castaway Cay and Lookout Cay

Why families choose her

Families should choose Donna because she genuinely cares about creating vacations that fit their unique needs, not just booking a trip. With nearly 30 years as a registered nurse, she’s built her career around listening, advocating, and paying attention to the details that make people feel supported — and she brings that same approach to travel planning.

Guests pay the same cruise fare as booking direct. Authorized agents are paid a commission by Disney Cruise Line.
