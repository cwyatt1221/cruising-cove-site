Bluey and Bingo are setting sail.

What to know about Bluey on Disney Cruise Line in 2026 — which ships, what the day looks like, and how to book without missing the programming.

Published July 30, 2026 · Entertainment · Wonder · Dream · Wish

If your family has a Disney+ subscription and a toddler, chances are Bluey is already on heavy rotation in your house. Good news for cruisers: the beloved Blue Heeler sisters are officially joining the Disney Cruise Line lineup in 2026, giving fans a brand-new way to experience the show at sea.

Where and when to find Bluey onboard

Bluey and her little sister Bingo made their Disney Cruise Line debut on the Disney Wonder in January 2026, and the program has since expanded to more ships and itineraries:

  • Disney Dream — sailing out of Fort Lauderdale, Florida, with Bluey programming since February 2026
  • Disney Wish — joining the lineup out of Port Canaveral starting June 2026
  • Disney Wonder — debut ship in January 2026; Bluey returns October 2026 from San Diego on select sailings

Important: Not every sailing on these ships includes Bluey programming. Double-check specific sailing dates when booking, or confirm with your travel advisor — Find an Agent.

What the experience actually looks like

Disney has built out a full day of Bluey-themed fun rather than a single quick photo op. Highlights include:

Wakey Wakey

A high-energy morning celebration packed with dancing and games, designed to get the whole family moving early in the day.

Character meet-and-greets

Face-to-face time with Bluey and Bingo, complete with photo opportunities.

Who’s Behind the Curtain

A family-friendly game show that wraps up with a dance party.

Pyjama Party

An evening wind-down featuring freeze dancing, interactive games, and a special character appearance, plus favorite games from the show, scavenger hunts, and crafts.

Guests should check the Disney Navigator App as soon as they board for the exact daily schedule, since timing can vary by sailing. Many families find the embarkation-day meet-and-greet — typically held in the early afternoon — is the least crowded opportunity of the whole trip.

Why this matters for families

Bluey has become one of the most-watched children’s shows in the world, and for families with kids roughly ages two to eight, meeting Bluey and Bingo carries the same excitement as meeting a classic Disney princess or superhero. Disney’s broader partnership with BBC Studios also means this is just the beginning — Bluey experiences are also rolling out at Disneyland and Walt Disney World in 2026, and a Bluey theatrical film is on the way in 2027.

Tips for booking a Bluey-themed cruise

  • Choose your ship based on your priorities. The Disney Dream offers longer 4–5 night sailings with rotational dining and the AquaDuck water coaster — a solid pick for a first Disney cruise. The Disney Wish offers shorter 3–4 night sailings with newer, more elaborate ship design.
  • Book a longer sailing if you can. A four-night (or longer) cruise gives your family a better shot at catching the full range of Bluey activities without feeling rushed.
  • Confirm the sailing includes Bluey programming. Since it’s offered on select sailings only, verify before you book.
  • Check the Navigator App on embarkation day. Schedules can shift slightly from sailing to sailing — that’s the most reliable way to plan around Bluey events.
