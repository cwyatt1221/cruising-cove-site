Logistics FAQ

Ports, money, and disembarkation.

The parts of a Disney cruise that happen off the ship — or at the very end — trip up a lot of first-timers. Here’s what to know.

Updated August 4, 2026 · Ports & logistics

Do I need foreign currency at the ports?

It depends on where you’re sailing. On most Bahamian and Caribbean itineraries, U.S. dollars are widely accepted and you likely won’t need to exchange currency. Still, it’s worth checking the specific ports on your itinerary ahead of time — Disney’s trip planning materials typically list what currency each port accepts, and it’s smart to be prepared if you plan on shopping off-ship.

What happens on disembarkation day?

Ships typically dock around 6:00 AM on the final morning, with disembarkation beginning around 7:30 AM. If everything goes smoothly, these times tend to be fairly consistent — though weather or logistical delays can push things back. Disney generally recommends not booking flights before 11:30 AM from most home ports, and aims to have all guests off the ship by around 9:00 AM. Check the specific guidance for your departure port, since timing can vary slightly.

Is a Disney cruise fun for adults without kids?

Yes. While Disney cruises don’t have the club-heavy party atmosphere of some other cruise lines, there’s plenty for adults to enjoy — adult-exclusive restaurants like Palo and Remy, adult-only pool and lounge areas, spa facilities, and evening entertainment. Many couples and childfree groups cruise Disney specifically for the polished service and theming, without ever setting foot in the kids’ areas.

Can weather or other circumstances change my itinerary?

Occasionally, yes. Weather conditions or other circumstances outside Disney’s control can require itinerary adjustments, such as skipping a port of call or altering the sailing route. These changes are made for guest safety and are typically communicated as early as possible once decided.
