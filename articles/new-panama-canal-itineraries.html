What’s new

More itineraries on Cruising Cove: Panama Canal & friends.

We’ve expanded beyond Bahamas and Caribbean staples — starting with Disney Magic Panama Canal sailings and new port guides for the stops that show up along the way.

August 11, 2026 · Itineraries & ports

Cruising Cove started with the ports and ships most families book first — Bahamas hops, Western and Eastern Caribbean favorites, Alaska, and the private islands. We’re growing the itinerary map. First up: Panama Canal repositioning cruises and the Pacific / Colombia ports that come with them.

What’s new

  • Panama Canal guide — transit-day planning (you stay aboard for the locks), 14-night Disney Magic sailings between San Diego and Galveston, age rules, packing, and seasickness notes.
  • Cabo San Lucas — tendering, Land’s End Arch boats, and Medano beach days.
  • Puerto Vallarta — Malecón walks, beach clubs, and traffic-aware timing.
  • Cartagena — walled old city, heat pacing, and pier-to-town transfer math.

You’ll also find Panama Canal templates in Compare sailings and the My Cruise planner, plus a packing section under Panama Canal.

Which Magic sailings are we talking about?

Disney has published two 14-night Panama Canal repositioning cruises on the Disney Magic:

  • Nov 6, 2026 — eastbound San Diego → Galveston (Cabo, Puerto Vallarta, canal transit, Cartagena, Grand Cayman, and sea days).
  • Apr 25, 2027 — westbound Galveston → San Diego (Cozumel, Cartagena, canal transit, Cabo, Ensenada, and sea days).

These are one-way sailings — you fly into one coast and home from the other. Confirm live itineraries and inventory with Disney or a travel agent; Cruising Cove doesn’t sell sailings.

Why this matters for planners

Panama Canal cruises aren’t a typical 3-night Bahamas hop. Expect ~8 sea days, a full canal-viewing day (not a Panama City pier afternoon), a 1-year minimum age rule, and open-jaw flights. The new guides are written for that reality — buffer math, heat, tendering, and what to prioritize so you don’t miss the locks.

Where to start

  • Browse the new Panama Canal region on the ports hub.
  • Read the Panama Canal overview, then open the port guides for your direction of travel.
  • Check embarkation logistics for San Diego and Galveston.
