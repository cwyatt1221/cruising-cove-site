Disney Wish vs Disney Treasure: which ship is better?

Same Wish-class bones — different themes. Fairytale enchantment on Wish; adventure and exploration on Treasure.

Published July 30, 2026 · Ships · Wish · Treasure

At first glance, the Disney Wish and Disney Treasure look nearly identical. Both are Wish-class ships with similar layouts, staterooms, pools, and entertainment spaces. Disney designed each around a very different theme, though — so the onboard experience feels surprisingly different.

Quick comparison

1. Overall theme

Disney Wish: fairytale magic

The Wish was inspired by classic fairytales and magical wishes — a Cinderella-inspired Grand Hall, princess-focused storytelling, elegant gold and blue décor, and an enchanted castle atmosphere. It feels like a floating Disney castle.

Disney Treasure: adventure awaits

The Treasure was built around Disney adventure stories: Aladdin, Coco, Jungle Cruise, Moana, Indiana Jones influences, and Adventureland inspiration. Instead of a castle, it feels like a Disney expedition.

Winner: Disney Treasure — the theme feels more unique and appeals to a broader range of guests.

2. Grand Hall

Disney Wish

Cinderella-inspired design with a Cinderella statue, sparkling chandelier, and royal colors.

Disney Treasure

Aladdin-inspired atmosphere with Agrabah influences, rich jewel tones, Jasmine-inspired décor, and adventure-themed details.

Winner: Tie — preference depends on princesses vs adventure stories.

3. Rotational dining

Disney Wish

Arendelle: A Frozen Dining Adventure

A dinner show featuring Anna, Elsa, Olaf, and live performances.

Worlds of Marvel

Interactive Marvel dining experience.

1923

Celebrates Walt Disney and the history of animation.

Disney Treasure

Plaza de Coco

One of Disney Cruise Line’s most ambitious dining shows — live musicians, story continuation from Coco, colorful performances, and a Mexican-inspired atmosphere.

Worlds of Marvel & 1923

Also available on Treasure.

Winner: Disney Treasure — most guests consider Plaza de Coco stronger than Arendelle for storytelling, music, and immersion. Deep dives: Wish dining · Treasure dining.

4. Adult-only spaces

Disney Wish — Star Wars: Hyperspace Lounge

Interactive windows, galactic views, signature cocktails, and Star Wars atmosphere — amazing for fans of the franchise.

Disney Treasure — Haunted Mansion Parlor

Ghostly effects, exclusive cocktails, themed décor, and interactive surprises — one of the most talked-about lounges in the fleet.

Winner: Disney Treasure — the Haunted Mansion Parlor is currently one of the most highly sought-after lounges afloat.

5. Entertainment

Disney Wish

Headline productions include Disney Seas the Adventure, The Little Mermaid, and Aladdin.

Disney Treasure

Headline productions include Disney Seas the Adventure, Beauty and the Beast, and Disney The Tale of Moana.

Winner: Disney Treasure — Disney The Tale of Moana is widely regarded as one of the most visually impressive productions Disney Cruise Line has created.

6. Kids’ areas

Both ships feature Marvel Super Hero Academy, Star Wars Cargo Bay, Imagineering Lab, and Oceaneer Club spaces that are nearly identical.

Winner: Tie — families with younger children won’t notice major differences.

7. Pools and outdoor areas

Almost identical designs — AquaMouse, multiple pools, family splash zones, and adult-only pool areas.

Winner: Tie — no meaningful difference.

8. Staterooms

Both offer split bathrooms, family-friendly layouts, ample storage, verandah options, and concierge accommodations. The rooms are essentially identical.

Winner: Tie

9. Disney fan appeal

Disney Wish

Best for princess lovers, first-time Disney cruisers, young families, and classic Disney fans.

Disney Treasure

Best for Disney enthusiasts, repeat cruisers, adventure fans, Pixar fans, and Haunted Mansion fans.

Winner: Disney Treasure — storytelling feels more sophisticated and offers more variety.

10. Which ship should you book?

Choose Disney Wish if

  • Your kids love princesses
  • You want a classic Disney experience
  • Frozen is a family favorite
  • It’s your first Disney cruise

Choose Disney Treasure if

  • You love Coco, Aladdin, Moana, or Haunted Mansion
  • You’ve already sailed on Disney Wish
  • You want the newest entertainment offerings
  • You prefer adventure over fairytale themes

Final verdict

Disney Wish is the perfect introduction to Disney Cruise Line.

Disney Treasure is the more immersive and ambitious ship. For first-timers, either is excellent. If prices are similar, Treasure gets the edge thanks to Plaza de Coco, the Haunted Mansion Parlor, and an adventure theme that lands with both kids and adults.
