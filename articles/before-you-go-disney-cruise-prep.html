Pre-cruise FAQ

Before you go: pre-cruise prep.

You’ve booked your Disney cruise — now what? Here’s what to sort out before you head to the port.

Updated August 4, 2026 · Before you sail

What documents do I need?

For most closed-loop cruises (round-trip from a U.S. port), a passport isn’t strictly required — a government-issued photo ID plus an original or certified copy of a birth certificate will usually work for U.S. citizens. That said, a passport is strongly recommended for everyone, since it’s the fastest way to get home if you need to fly out of a foreign port for any reason (medical emergency, missed ship, weather disruption). Check expiration dates well ahead of time — some countries require passports valid for six months beyond your travel dates. More detail: passport FAQ.

What should I pack for a Disney cruise?

A few often-overlooked essentials:

  • Layers. Ship air conditioning tends to run cold, especially in dining rooms and theaters.
  • A lightweight stroller, even for toddlers who normally walk — the ships are long, and there’s a lot of walking between venues.
  • Swim diapers for little ones, since most main pools require them and they can sell out in the ship’s shop.
  • A portable phone charger — you’ll be using the Navigator app constantly, and outlets aren’t always close by.
  • Formal or semi-formal attire if your cruise includes a dress-up night, plus casual “cruise casual” outfits for regular dinners (shorts and t-shirts are generally fine, though tank tops and swimwear aren’t allowed in dining rooms).

What time do I board, and what time do I need to be on the ship?

Boarding typically begins around 11:00 AM, with boarding numbers assigned based on your chosen arrival window. By around 12:30 PM, boarding usually opens up with no number required. The one hard deadline to remember: you must be onboard by the published all-aboard time on your documents (often around 4:00 PM on many sailings) — miss that, and the ship leaves without you. Confirm your Port Arrival Time and all-aboard in the app for your sailing.

What else should I do before I leave home?

Download the Disney Cruise Line Navigator app in advance — it’s how you’ll check kids in and out of the club, view daily activities, browse menus, and check wait times once you’re onboard. It works over the ship’s Wi-Fi, so download it before you go rather than trying to get it once you’ve boarded.

Also lock money dates with the sailing timeline and finish online check-in when your Castaway Club window opens.
