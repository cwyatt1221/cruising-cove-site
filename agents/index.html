— The part people miss

Why it’s free for you

Disney Cruise Line pays travel agencies a commission for bringing them a booking. That comes from Disney’s marketing budget, not your fare. You pay the exact same price whether you book direct or through a specialist — the difference is having an advocate between deposit and sail-away.

— What a good agent does

The real value

  • Price monitoring — if the rate drops, they fight for the rebook or credit.
  • Booking windows — Palo, Remy, cabanas, and popular excursions sell out fast.
  • A human when plans break — flights, docs, last-minute changes.
  • Group coordination — multi-cabin paperwork without the group-chat chaos.
— Credentials

Look for EarMarked by Disney

Agents at EarMarked agencies complete Disney’s College of Disney Knowledge and stay certified annually. Cruising Cove features Disney-specialist agents — always confirm their authorization when you connect.
