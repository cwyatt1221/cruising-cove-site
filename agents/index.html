Booking help

A travel agent costs you nothing extra.

This is the part most first-time cruisers don't know: booking through a Disney-specialist agent costs exactly the same as booking direct. The commission comes from Disney, not from you.

— The part people miss

Why it's free

Disney Cruise Line pays travel agencies a commission for bringing them a booking — it's built into how Disney sells cruises, the same way it works with hotels and airlines. That commission comes out of Disney's marketing budget, not out of your fare. You pay the exact same price whether you book directly with Disney or through a specialist agent. The only difference is whether someone is working on your behalf for the year-plus between booking and sailing, or whether you're doing all of it yourself.

— What a good agent actually does

The real value

  • Price monitoring. If your cruise's price drops after you book, a good agent tracks it and gets you rebooked at the lower rate or the difference applied — something you'd have to catch and request yourself otherwise.
  • Booking-window strategy. Palo, Remy, cabanas, and popular excursions sell out within minutes of the window opening. An experienced agent knows the exact timing for your Castaway Club tier and can have things ready to book the second it opens.
  • Someone to call when something goes wrong. A cancelled flight, a question about documentation, a last-minute itinerary change — you have a person, not a call center queue.
  • They've actually sailed it. Disney-specialist agents complete Disney's own College of Disney Knowledge training and many have sailed dozens of times. That's real, current, first-hand knowledge most first-time cruisers don't have access to anywhere else.
  • Group and multi-cabin coordination. If you're traveling with extended family across several staterooms, one person coordinating everyone's paperwork, dining, and payments is worth a lot on its own.
— How to know they're legitimate

What to look for

Disney rebranded its agent credentialing program to "EarMarked by Disney – Authorized Travel Agency" in early 2026 (previously called "Authorized Disney Vacation Planner"). Agents at EarMarked agencies complete Disney's College of Disney Knowledge and pass annual exams to stay certified. Look for that designation, or ask directly whether an agent is Disney-authorized before booking.
