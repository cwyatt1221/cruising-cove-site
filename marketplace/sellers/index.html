How it works

Applications are free. If you're one of the 10 founding partners we select, you get a 30-day free trial — no card charged during the trial, cancel anytime with nothing owed. After 30 days, it's $39/month, locked at that rate for your first year.

  • What you get: a featured shop profile in the Cruising Cove marketplace directory, a credited link to your Etsy shop, and exposure to families actively planning a Disney cruise.
  • How billing works: if you're selected, we'll follow up directly to confirm your trial start date. No payment during the 30-day trial — after that, we'll send you a simple monthly payment request (PayPal or Venmo).
  • Only 10 spots, this year only. Founding partners lock in this rate and get first right of renewal as the directory grows.

Eligible product categories

To keep things simple and licensing-clean, featured placement is limited to items that don't use Disney characters or artwork:

Packing organizers Door & cabin magnets Lanyards & pin displays Luggage tags Generic nautical decor Port-specific items Personalized name signs (no characters) Fish extender bases (no character art)

Character merchandise is still very welcome as a free, user-submitted listing elsewhere on the site — it just isn't eligible for this featured program.
