Themed sailings

Not every sailing is the same sailing.

Certain dates on certain ships get a full extra layer of theming. Here's what's running in 2026, and which ship and itinerary each one lives on.

— Booking

How themed sailings work

Halloween, Very Merrytime, Marvel Day at Sea, and Pixar Day at Sea are overlays on select sailings — not separate tickets. You book a normal cruise on an eligible date; the theming is included. High-demand holiday weeks sell out early, and Castaway Club booking windows still control when you can reserve cabanas, dining, and Port Adventures.

  • Activity windows: same Castaway Club calendar as any other sailing — booking window helper.
  • Final payment: still due on Disney’s timeline before most online activity booking — deposit & final payment.
  • Packing: costumes are optional but common on Halloween / Very Merrytime — see the Halloween packing section and Pirate Night guide.
— Superheroes at sea

Marvel Day at Sea

Marvel Day at Sea returns on the Disney Magic for 4- and 5-night Western Caribbean itineraries out of Galveston, Texas starting January 2026 — the first time this event sails from Galveston (Magic has hosted Marvel Day on other itineraries since 2017). Over 30 heroes and villains appear across the day, including Spider-Man, Captain America, Captain Marvel, Thor, Loki, and Black Panther — plus a USO-style tribute show honoring Captain America in Fathoms and warrior training with Okoye in the atrium. The day builds to a deck party themed around a clash between heroes and villains.

Book early if Marvel is the reason you’re sailing — these dates are easy to miss once verandah inventory thins out. Confirm your specific voyage is labeled Marvel Day at Sea in Disney’s booking tools before you deposit.

— Pixar pals

Pixar Day at Sea

Pixar Day at Sea returns on the Disney Fantasy, sailing 5-night Bahamian itineraries from Port Canaveral, Florida, stopping at Nassau, Castaway Cay, and Lookout Cay at Lighthouse Point. Expect appearances from beloved Pixar characters throughout the day, capped off with a Pixar Pals Celebration deck party in the evening.

— Seasonal favorites

Halloween on the High Seas & Very Merrytime Cruises

Halloween on the High Seas runs on select September–October sailings across the fleet — Pumpkin Tree lighting, Mickey’s Mouse-querade, trick-or-treating, seasonal menus, and Halloween character costumes. Destiny joins the celebration for 2026; full rundown: Halloween on the Destiny.

Very Merrytime Cruises bring holiday theming to U.S.-based DCL ships in the winter season — tree lightings, holiday characters, and festive entertainment layered onto regular itineraries. Treat these like peak holiday demand: book earlier than a random January sailing, and expect higher fares around Christmas and New Year’s weeks.

— Character-themed sailings

Bluey & other family favorites

2026 also brings Bluey and Bingo–themed experiences to select sailings on the Disney Dream, Disney Wish, and returning Disney Wonder (San Diego, fall 2026), aimed at families with younger kids. Details: Bluey on Disney Cruise Line in 2026.

What isn't currently running

— Status check

Star Wars Day at Sea has run on Disney Cruise Line in the past and is frequently requested by fans, but is not part of the confirmed 2026 lineup as of this writing. Worth verifying current availability before booking a sailing specifically for it.
