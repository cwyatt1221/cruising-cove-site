Themed sailings

Not every sailing is the same sailing.

Certain dates on certain ships get a full extra layer of theming. Here's what's running in 2026, and which ship and itinerary each one lives on.

— Superheroes at sea

Marvel Day at Sea

For the first time, Marvel Day at Sea moved to the Disney Magic, sailing 4- and 5-night Western Caribbean itineraries out of Galveston, Texas starting January 2026. Over 30 heroes and villains appear across the day, including Spider-Man, Captain America, Captain Marvel, Thor, Loki, and Black Panther — plus a USO-style tribute show honoring Captain America in Fathoms and warrior training with Okoye in the atrium. The day builds to a deck party themed around a clash between heroes and villains.

— Pixar pals

Pixar Day at Sea

Pixar Day at Sea returns on the Disney Fantasy, sailing 5-night Bahamian itineraries from Port Canaveral, Florida, stopping at Nassau, Castaway Cay, and Lookout Cay at Lighthouse Point. Expect appearances from beloved Pixar characters throughout the day, capped off with a Pixar Pals Celebration deck party in the evening.

— Seasonal favorites

Halloween on the High Seas & Very Merrytime Cruises

Halloween on the High Seas runs during the fall season across the fleet, with themed decor, character costumes, and trick-or-treating activities for kids. Very Merrytime Cruises bring holiday theming to U.S.-based DCL ships during the winter season — tree lightings, holiday character appearances, and festive entertainment. Both are seasonal add-ons to regular sailings rather than a separate ship or itinerary.

— Character-themed sailings

Bluey & other family favorites

2026 also brings Bluey and Bingo–themed experiences to select sailings on the Disney Dream and Disney Wish, aimed at families with younger kids.

— Worth knowing

What isn't currently running

— Status check

Star Wars Day at Sea has run on Disney Cruise Line in the past and is frequently requested by fans, but is not part of the confirmed 2026 lineup as of this writing. Worth verifying current availability before booking a sailing specifically for it.
