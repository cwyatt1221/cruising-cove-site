Onboard Entertainment

What there is to do once the sun goes down.

Stage shows, character moments, fireworks, and the deck party everyone talks about.

Stage Shows

Every ship runs original stage productions in its main theater across the sailing — book-quality sets, live performers, and a rotating lineup that varies by ship. Popular showings can fill up, so check timing early in your cruise.

Scheduled meet-and-greet times run throughout the cruise, plus a free character phone call you can schedule before you even set sail.

Fireworks at Sea

A signature nighttime deck event on many sailings — one of the few places you'll see a real fireworks show launched from a ship at sea. Deck space fills up, so stake out a spot early if you want a clear view.

Pirate Night

A themed deck party night with costumes, dancing, and a nautical takeover of the whole ship — one of the most anticipated nights of the cruise for a lot of families.
