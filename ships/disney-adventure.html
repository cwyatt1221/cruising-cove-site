— The ship

Overview

The Disney Adventure completed her maiden voyage on March 10, 2026 from Singapore's Marina Bay Cruise Centre — Disney Cruise Line's first-ever homeport in Asia, after a launch delay pushed her back from the original December 2025 date. Built on a hull originally intended for an unrelated cruise project, she's by far the largest ship in the DCL fleet, sized closer to a mainstream mega-ship than any other Disney vessel.

She currently sails 3- and 4-night itineraries from Singapore, with 5-night sailings added through 2026.

— Where you'll sleep

Staterooms

Staterooms carry subtle Little Mermaid–inspired touches, and a set of garden-view staterooms sit within the ship's Imagination Garden themed area — a layout unique to this ship among the fleet. Full category and pricing detail is still emerging as the ship settles into regular service.

Dining

Dining information for the Adventure is still being confirmed as she settles into regular sailings; DCL's standard rotational-dining concept applies fleet-wide, alongside adult-exclusive and quick-service venues distributed across her seven themed areas.

— Worth knowing

Signature features

  • Seven themed lands, including Marvel Landing, Toy Story Place, and the Moana-inspired Wayfinder Bay
  • Ironcycle Test Run — a Marvel-themed rollercoaster stretching over 250 meters, the longest rollercoaster at sea
  • Disney Cruise Line's first homeport in Asia
  • By far the largest ship in the fleet, with roughly 6,000-passenger capacity

Easter eggs & hidden details

— Still being catalogued

As the fleet's newest and largest ship, detailed hidden-Mickey and Easter-egg documentation for the Disney Adventure is still thin compared to the rest of the fleet — she only began regular sailings in March 2026. What's confirmed so far: each of her seven themed lands (Imagination Garden, Marvel Landing, Toy Story Place, San Fransokyo Street, Town Square, Wayfinder Bay, and Disney Discovery Reef) carries its own dense layer of thematic detail, consistent with how Disney has built out every other ship in the fleet.
