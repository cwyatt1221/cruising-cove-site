— The ship

Overview

The Disney Adventure completed her maiden voyage on March 10, 2026 from Singapore's Marina Bay Cruise Centre — Disney Cruise Line's first-ever homeport in Asia, after a launch delay pushed her back from the original December 2025 date. Built on a hull originally intended for an unrelated cruise project, she's by far the largest ship in the DCL fleet, sized closer to a mainstream mega-ship than any other Disney vessel.

She currently sails 3- and 4-night itineraries from Singapore, with 5-night sailings added through 2026.
