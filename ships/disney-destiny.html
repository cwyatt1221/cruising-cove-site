— The ship

Overview

The Disney Destiny set sail in November 2025 as the third Wish-class ship, themed around the conflict between Disney heroes and villains. She sails shorter 4- and 5-night Bahamas and Western Caribbean itineraries from Port Everglades in Fort Lauderdale — a good fit for first-time cruisers or families working with a tighter vacation window. In 2026, she was named to Travel + Leisure's "It List" as one of the year's best new ships.

— Where you'll sleep

Staterooms

Staterooms carry artwork themed to Hercules, Mulan, Merida, and Sorcerer Mickey from Fantasia. Concierge suites and staterooms lean into the Fantasia theme, and the Royal Suites include a Hercules-themed suite with Greco-Roman details and an Incredibles-themed "IncrediSuite" with a mid-century modern look. The two-story Destiny Tower Suite, set in the forward funnel, is themed to Iron Man. As on every DCL ship, the split-bathroom layout and connecting-door options run throughout.

— Where you'll eat

Dining

Rotational dining includes Pride Lands (the main dining room, themed to The Lion King), Worlds of Marvel, and 1923. Adult-exclusive venues are Palo and Enchanté, matching the newer Wish-class dining structure. The Sanctum, a Doctor Strange–themed lounge on Deck 3, hosts trivia and drawing classes by day and live music at night.

— Look closer

Easter eggs & hidden details

The Destiny leans hard into her heroes-and-villains theme with genuinely clever details:

  • Hero Minnie appears on the bow — her first appearance on any Disney Cruise Line ship. On the stern, Spider-Man swings into action — the first Marvel character ever featured on a DCL ship's hull.
  • Lightning bolts worked into the ship's decor and carpeting aren't just decoration — every one of them points forward, toward the bow, symbolizing motion and nodding to the villain side of the theme.
  • Hidden Mickeys woven into the stateroom carpeting follow the same rule: wherever Mickey points, it's toward the front of the ship.
  • Because the Destiny is the seventh ship in the DCL fleet, the number seven shows up as a recurring motif — including seven heroes represented in certain spaces, and seven villains in others.
  • The Sanctum, the ship's Doctor Strange–themed lounge, includes details pulled directly from the films — the Cloak of Levitation and spellbooks from Kamar-Taj among them.
