— The ship

Overview

Disney Cruise Line named the Disney Believe on March 18, 2026 — the ninth ship in the fleet and the fourth Wish-class vessel after Wish, Treasure, and Destiny. Steel cutting began August 8, 2025 at Meyer Werft in Papenburg, Germany. Delivery is expected in 2027, with a late-2027 debut.

Disney has described the ship’s theme as promise and possibility, with inspirations drawn from Encanto, Frozen, Moana, Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs, and The Little Mermaid. A specific homeport and itinerary slate have not been announced; more detail is expected as construction progresses (Disney has teased further reveals later in 2026).
