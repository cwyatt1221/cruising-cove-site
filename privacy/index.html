Privacy at Cruising Cove

How we handle the information you share with us — and what we don’t do with it.

— Our commitment

We don’t sell or hand off your personal details

Cruising Cove is an independent planning site. When you create an account, leave a comment, request an agent, or submit a form, we use that information only to run and improve Cruising Cove — for example, to keep you signed in, show your community posts, or connect you with a travel agent you asked to hear from. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not provide your account or form details to third parties for their marketing. We never ask for Disney Cruise Line or MyDisney login credentials.

If you request a conversation with a travel agent listed on Cruising Cove, we share only what you submitted in that request with the agent you chose — so they can follow up with you. That handoff is at your direction, not a general data sale or mailing-list transfer.

Questions about this policy? Email cassondra@cruisingcove.com.

Cruising Cove is an independent planning guide and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by The Walt Disney Company, Disney Cruise Line, or any of their affiliates.
